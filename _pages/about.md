# Junteng Liu

I am a first-year PhD candidate in the HKUST NLP Group. I graduated from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024. My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning.

My research interests include:

- LLM reasoning and reinforcement learning
- Hallucination in vision-language models (VLMs)
- LLM truthfulness and interpretability

## Academic Background

- **Ph.D. in Computer Science**, Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (2024–Present), HKUST NLP Group. Ph.D. supervisor: Prof. Junxian He.
- **B.Eng.**, Shanghai Jiao Tong University (2020–2024); graduated in June 2024. Prof. Junxian He previously advised me during my undergraduate studies at SJTU.
